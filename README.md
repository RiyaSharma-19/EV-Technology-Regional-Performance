# EV Technology & Regional Performance

**Data Analytics with Tableau – Capstone Project**

**Author:** Riya Sharma, Medicaps University · **Team ID:** NA

An EV Technology Dashboard in Tableau, built on 199,999 cleaned electric vehicle records. It combines regional sales with battery size, energy efficiency, AC charging time, motor power and ownership cost.

## Key findings
- **Regions:** each of the 7 market regions holds about 14% of sales, and sales stay steady from 2018 to 2026.
- **Batteries:** NMC and LFP are the top battery types in every region.
- **Heavy vehicles:** Electric Buses (300 kWh) and Electric Trucks (225 kWh) have the biggest batteries and use over 1,000 Wh per km.
- **Charging:** AC charging takes about 11.3 hours for every battery chemistry.
- **Power and cost:** AWD motors are about 54% more powerful than FWD/RWD, and insurance cost rises almost linearly with price.
